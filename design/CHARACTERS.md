# VOID TRANSIT — Character Reference

## The Player

- **Identity**: Unknown at start. Cryo Unit 37. Does not remember their own name.
- **Role**: Crew member (role TBD). Not a colonist.
- **Pod**: Unit 37, Cryogenic Suspension Bay, Deck D.
- **Revival**: Emergency thaw — not scheduled, not self-initiated. Something woke them.
- **State at opening**: Naked, wet with cryoprotectant gel, weightless, amnesiac, cold, nauseous. Health 65/100 (cryo sickness).

The player discovers their identity gradually through the game. They are NOT Chen Wei-Lin.

## Chen Wei-Lin

- **Role**: Navigation Officer
- **Pod**: C-447, Cryogenic Suspension Bay, Deck D.
- **Revival**: Self-initiated manual override, Mission Year 17.4 (almost 2 years before the player wakes).
- **Status**: Missing. Not in her pod. Not anywhere on the ship (that the player can find).

### What Chen did while awake (discovered through clues):

1. **Self-elevated her security clearance** (crew manifest shows "ELEVATED (SELF-AUTHORIZED)")
2. **Modified the reactor** — handwritten notes on the reactor console; magnetic confinement parameters adjusted
3. **Built an antenna** — hand-fabricated array on the hull exterior, wired into the power grid, aimed at coordinates 0.3 light-years away where nothing should exist
4. **Sent encrypted transmissions** — unauthorized, using personal encryption key ("CHEN-PERSONAL"), transmitted at Mission Year 18.6
5. **Received a response** — data packet in the comms buffer, from the same impossible coordinates, received 11 hours before the player wakes, header reads "JANUS-7 RESPONSE // PRIORITY ALPHA"
6. **Accessed the player's pod** — Chen's jumpsuit (name patch: CHEN) and personal items are in the player's pod compartment. Why? This is a mystery.
7. **Left clues everywhere** — star charts with annotations not in the captain's handwriting, navigation chair adjusted for someone shorter, ladder rungs worn from hundreds of transits, a black hair in crew quarters

### Why the jumpsuit is in the player's pod

This is deliberately mysterious. Possible explanations (to be resolved in-story):
- Chen moved it there deliberately before disappearing
- Chen was preparing the player's pod for revival
- Chen and the player had a connection before departure

## Captain Lindqvist

- **Role**: Ship's Captain
- **Status**: In cryo (Pod assignment TBD). Has not been revived.
- **Personality**: Precise, careful, believes in doing things right. Swedish background.
- **Clues**: Her quarters are locked (need captain's key). Dress uniform pressed and ready. Photo of family on Baltic shore. Her handwriting is on the emergency engineering kit card.

## Other Characters

All 2,847 colonists and remaining crew are in cryo. The player is alone on the ship — with the growing evidence that someone else was awake before them, and that someone may have contacted something that shouldn't exist.
